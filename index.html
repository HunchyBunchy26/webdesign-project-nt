Places to Visit

The city's most important landmark is Daugavpils Fortress, a vast 19th-century military complex and the only preserved fortress of its kind in Eastern Europe.
Daugavpils is also a gateway to the natural landscapes of Latgale, a region known for its lakes, forests and traditional villages. The city is also recognised for Church Hill, a site gathering four distinct religious buildings, and the walking routes along the Daugava River.

Where To Stay

The City Centre is the most practical choice for easy access to restaurants, local transport and landmarks like Unity Square.
If you prefer a quieter stay focussed on history and culture, look for accommodation near Daugavpils Fortress and the Mark Rothko Art Centre.
We recommend you stay at least 4 days in order to fully appreciate everything Daugavpils has to offer.

Where To Eat

Daugavpils is home to a burgeoning culinary scene, with a host of charming cafes, trendy bistros, and traditional restaurants. With its unique blend of culinary traditions and local delicacies, Daugavpils offers an unforgettable experience for foodies.
So, if you're looking for an immersive gastronomic experience, don't miss out on "Daugavpils food tours" and prepare your taste buds for an unforgettable journey through this vibrant Latvian city.
